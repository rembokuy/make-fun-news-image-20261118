Seven stories that defined the day — from Shenzhen to deep space
Diplomacy — Lead Story

Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen on the eve of a first three-way summit with Trump

China’s Xi Jinping and Russia’s Vladimir Putin opened talks in Shenzhen on Wednesday, a day before their first-ever trilateral summit with U.S. President Donald Trump. Xi pledged to stand against trade protectionism; Putin attacked Western sanctions.

The Gulf

Iran fires drones at Kuwait and Bahrain after U.S. strike

Iran said its drones targeted the U.S.-hosted Ali Al Salem Air Base in Kuwait and launched fresh attacks on Bahrain, a day after U.S. jets hit a missile site on Iran’s Qeshm Island. Kuwait and Bahrain said their defences intercepted the attacks.

U.S. Politics

Peltola wins Alaska Senate race; Democrats take 56–44 majority

Democrat Mary Peltola defeated two-term Republican Sen. Dan Sullivan, the Associated Press projected, settling the last Senate race of the midterms. Peltola, with 50.1% of first-choice votes, becomes the first Alaska Native in the U.S. Senate.

Tech — Markets

Anthropic prices its IPO at $83 a share, above its range

The maker of the Claude AI models priced its market debut at $83 a share, above the $70–$78 range, in an enlarged offering of 980 million shares worth about $81.3 billion. Trading on the Nasdaq under ANT starts Thursday.

Tech — Earnings

Nvidia posts $115.8bn quarter as Huang era ends

Nvidia reported third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% on a year earlier, with earnings of $2.68 a share — its first report since founder Jensen Huang stepped down. Its board ruled out reinstating him as chief executive.

Space

Voyager 1 reaches one light-day from Earth

NASA’s Voyager 1 became the first human-made object to reach one light-day from Earth — about 16.1 billion miles — at 5:16 a.m. EST, nearly 50 years after its 1977 launch. Its radio signals now take a full day to reach home.

Health

U.S. loses measles-elimination status after 26 years

The Pan American Health Organization revoked the status the U.S. had held since 2000, after measles spread without a break for more than a year. Over 5,400 cases were recorded in 2026, the most in decades.
